# Polyform Whitepaper

## Vision

Markets on Solana move faster than any one person can follow. Polyform exists to give that motion a shape: one calm surface where the busiest tokens of the day are ranked, compared and checked before anyone acts.

## Mission

- Show what is trading, and how heavily, without noise
- Keep every claim traceable to a stated source
- Leave unconfirmed facts visibly empty instead of filling them with guesses
